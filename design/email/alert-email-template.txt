--- notes ---

Not a stripped copy of the HTML. This is the version a pager gateway or a
text-only client shows, so links are SPELLED OUT rather than hidden behind
anchors, and {brand.header} prints the app name in text even on installs where
the HTML leaves that job to the wordmark art.

"Subject", not "Device": plenty of alerts are about the app itself (a capacity
escalation, a failed backup, the host's own CPU) rather than about a monitored
device — labelling the server as a "Device" made those read like they were
about somebody's switch.

Like the HTML, no {message} line: it restates {trigger.summary}. The two bodies
must stay in step, or an operator editing one wonders why the other differs.

Run pruneEmptyTextLines on the RENDERED body: it drops "Label:" lines with
nothing after the colon and collapses the blank-line runs that leaves behind.
